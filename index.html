"Northwest Dissident" is a community of supporters dedicated to the PSS Sleman football club. The name "Northwest Dissident" describes the enthusiasm and courage of its members in supporting their favorite team.

"Northwest Dissident" is not just a supporter group, but also a family for its members. We provide a platform for the exchange of ideas, experiences and social activities that strengthen relationships among fellow football fans.
